GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

Is Ken Mirror Screen really free?

Yes. Both apps are completely free to download and use, with no account or subscription. Source code and licenses are included in the source download.

Will it work on my devices?

The Android APK requires Android 10 or newer. The Windows download is for 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. Keep both devices on the same network and allow the PC app through the firewall on your trusted private network.

Is the mirroring instant?

There is always some capture, encoding, and network delay. Ken Mirror includes a Low delay option and quality presets up to 60 fps. Results depend on your devices and connection; frame rate and latency are not guaranteed.

Why is an app’s sound or video missing?

Some Android apps block playback capture or protect their video. Ken Mirror respects these restrictions. Enable Share tablet sound before connecting for supported audio.

Why do I see borders in Fit mode?

Your tablet and monitor may have different proportions. Fit preserves the entire picture without distortion. Whole screen fills the monitor but can stretch the image. Crop to fill and zoom deliberately trim edges.

How do I hide the controls?

Press H to hide or show the toolbar, and F to toggle fullscreen. You can also move the pointer to reveal the auto-hiding controls. Press Escape to leave fullscreen.
